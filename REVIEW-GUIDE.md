# Review attribution guide

The site can use reviews from Google Business, direct client messages or approved testimonials. Accuracy matters more than making an attribution sound impressive.

## Recommended structure

- **Name:** the exact public display name used on Google, or the name the client approved.
- **Context:** a separate factual label such as `Chiropractic table restoration`, `Residential sofa client` or `Dental clinic owner`.
- **Company:** include only when the company relationship is public or the client has approved its use.
- **Source:** `Google review`, `Direct client review` or another accurate description.
- **URL:** use the individual Google review share URL when available. Otherwise link to Nora's complete Google Business review profile and label the action simply `View on Google`.
- **Quote:** preserve the original wording. Minor trimming is acceptable only when it does not change meaning; use an ellipsis when text is removed.

Do not change `Jane Smith` to `Jane Smith — ABC Dental` based only on your assumption. A safe alternative is:

> Jane Smith  
> Google review · Dental chair project

Reviews remain hard-coded in the site so they load quickly and can include verified project context. The Google source label and link make their origin clear; they are not presented as a live Google feed or an automatically synchronized rating.

## Homepage balance

Clinical reviews should appear first because dental, medical and chiropractic work is the highest-priority audience. Follow with residential and commercial reviews. Restaurant, gym, bowling-centre and other specialty venue work all belongs within the commercial group.
